# Surface: CRM app shell and home (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Greenfield associates and sourcers, phone and desktop in equal measure. Job on this surface: see what must be done today on deals and developer follow-ups, act in one gesture, and read the pipeline and developer answers at a glance. Constraints: site identity pinned (PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments); single-file vanilla app; existing pages and behaviours preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The CRM opens on the day's work, not on portfolio vanity numbers. Refuses the category-default dashboard of big KPI tiles and charts with the to-do hidden below.

OWN-WORLD: greenfield.plus identity: ink rail, deep green ground, mint for primary action and selection, light paper panels for dense data, Archivo for page titles and key numbers, Inter Tight for everything else, mono only for small kickers. Zoning codes as plaques coloured by zone family. Red late, amber today, mint upcoming.

STORY: The user lands, sees how many follow-ups are late or due today, clears them from the list (call, WhatsApp, open, done), then glances at the pipeline and the latest developer answers.

Phone: logo header, three counters, the "A faire" list, bottom tabs and the + button.
